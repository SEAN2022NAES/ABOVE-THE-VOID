## 使用建议 / Usage Notes

1. **不要**在邮件中承诺价格或独家条款，所有报价以书面条款草案为准。/ Never commit to a price or
   exclusivity in the first e-mail; quotes must come from a written term sheet.
2. 保留全部邮件往来（含对方自述的使用场景与规模），可作为后续主张损失的证据。/
   Keep all correspondence, including the other party's own description of scope and scale, as evidence.
3. 授权凭证建议单独成文（授权书 / 许可协议签署件），并在邮件中明确授权的生效日期、
   范围、期限与凭证编号。/ Issue the licence certificate as a separate document stating the effective date,
   scope, term and certificate number.
4. 涉及金额较大或独占授权时，建议由律师审阅协议后再签署。/ For large or exclusive deals, have a lawyer
   review the agreement before signing.
