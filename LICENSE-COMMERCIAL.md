## 3. 授权方式 / How Licences Are Structured

商业授权**按项目、按期限、按范围分别协商**，不提供统一价格表，也不适用默认条款。

Commercial licences are **negotiated per project, per term and per scope**. There is no public price list and no
default terms.

1. **按项目 / Per project**：一个授权对应一个明确的产品或项目，跨项目使用需另行授权。/
   One licence covers one defined product or project; use in another project requires a separate licence.
2. **按期限 / Per term**：可选一次性永久授权，或按年（1 年 / 2 年 / 3 年）授权，到期续约。/
   Either a one-off perpetual licence or an annual licence (1, 2 or 3 years), renewable on expiry.
3. **按范围 / Per scope**：按发行渠道数量、平台数量、开发者席位数、发行地域、是否独占、是否含源码交付等维度
   分档定价。/ Priced by number of channels, number of platforms, developer seats, territory, exclusivity and
   whether source code is delivered.
4. **收入分成（可选）/ Revenue share (optional)**：对于小体量项目，可采用「较低预付款 + 流水分成」的方式，
   分成基数、比例、结算周期与审计方式在协议中约定。/ For smaller projects, a low upfront fee plus a revenue
   share may be used; the base, rate, settlement cycle and audit method are set in the agreement.

**授权流程 / Process**

1. 你提交商业授权询问（见 `docs/COMMERCIAL-LICENSE-INQUIRY.md` 模板）。/ You send an enquiry (template in
   `docs/COMMERCIAL-LICENSE-INQUIRY.md`).
2. 双方确认使用场景、分发渠道、预计规模与预算区间。/ Both sides confirm the use case, channels, expected
   scale and budget range.
3. 作者出具书面报价与条款草案。/ The author issues a written quote and term sheet.
4. 签署书面许可协议并支付约定费用。/ The licence agreement is signed and the agreed fee is paid.
5. 作者出具授权凭证（授权书 / 许可协议签署件），你方可开始商业使用。/ The author issues the licence
   certificate (authorisation letter or signed agreement) and commercial use may begin.

⚠️ **在收到书面授权凭证之前，任何商业使用均属未授权使用。**
⚠️ **Until the written licence certificate is received, any commercial use is unauthorised.**

## 5. 常见问题 / FAQ

**Q：我自己做游戏，想免费发布，需要授权吗？**
A：只要不涉及任何直接或间接盈利（无付费、无广告、无内购、无平台分成、无赞助），属于非商业用途，无需授权，
保留署名即可。一旦产生任何形式的收益或商业目的，即需授权。
**Q: I want to publish my own game for free. Do I need a licence?**
A: If there is no direct or indirect monetisation (no payment, ads, IAP, platform revenue share or sponsorship),
it is non-commercial and no licence is needed — attribution is required. Any revenue or commercial purpose
requires a licence.

**Q：公司内部做一个演示、原型或培训材料，算商业用途吗？**
A：算。公司内部生产性使用属于商业用途，需事先取得授权。
**Q: Is an internal demo, prototype or training material inside a company commercial?**
A: Yes. Internal production use in a company is commercial and requires a licence.

**Q：学校里讲课、学生做课程作业可以用吗？**
A：可以，属于非商业用途，免费；需保留署名，且课程本身不得收费。
**Q: May I use it for a school lecture or student coursework?**
A: Yes, that is non-commercial and free, with attribution, provided the course itself is not charged for.

**Q：我可以把代码提交到自己的开源仓库吗？**
A：在不含任何盈利入口、保留署名与许可文件的前提下可以；但不得改用其他许可重新发布。
**Q: May I put the code in my own open-source repository?**
A: Yes, without any monetisation and with attribution and the licence file retained; you may not re-release it
under a different licence.

**Q：可以只买一小部分模块（例如相机控制器）吗？**
A：可以，授权范围可按模块拆分，按范围定价。
**Q: May I license only a small module, such as the camera controller?**
A: Yes. Scope can be split by module and priced accordingly.
